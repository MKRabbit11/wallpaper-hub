Quality and power graphics level, frame rate, resolution
Graphics
How much detail the animated scenes draw. Eco is cool and quiet; Ultra looks best and uses the most power.
Render scale
Draws the animated scenes bigger than your screen and shrinks them to fit, so edges and fine detail are sharper. 200% is like 4K on a 1080p screen. Higher uses much more graphics power. It does not change how videos look.
Frame rate limit
Use your own limit, any number from 30 to 500, instead of the Graphics setting. Lower is cooler and quieter. A wallpaper can never run faster than your monitor's refresh rate, and videos play at their own speed. Off by default.
Give games more CPU
Runs the wallpaper at a lower priority, so a game gets the processor first. Off by default.
Resolution
How sharply it is drawn. Lower uses less graphics power. Applies to scenes, YouTube, videos and GIFs.
Switch graphics automatically
Drops to Eco on battery, and one step lower while the graphics card is busy. Off by default.
Show a live readout
How much CPU and graphics power the wallpaper itself uses, and its real frame rate. Off by default.

Overlay on top of games

Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+P. Shows over borderless or windowed-fullscreen games (not exclusive fullscreen).
CPU temperature: Windows keeps it private from normal programs, and reading it needs a special driver, so this app does not install one. If you run the free HWiNFO (turn on Settings > Shared Memory Support), the temperature appears here automatically.
Game FPS: Windows does not let a program see another game's frame rate. MSI Afterburner (with RivaTuner) measures it, and this app shows it while RivaTuner is running and a game is open.

Sharpness
How a small GIF or video is enlarged. Smooth hides the dots in GIFs; Sharp adds crispness.
Screen type

Desktop icons fade them away so the wallpaper shows
Fade away desktop icons
Icons fade away and only the one under your mouse shows. Icon Fade is built in; your icons always come back when you turn this off or quit.
Hide completely (stronger)
If faint ghosts of your icons still show on your screen, turn this on. It also makes the icon names black while hidden. Your names go back to normal when icons show again.
Fully invisible (HDR screens, experimental)
For screens with HDR on, where icons still show faintly. The icon layer is made completely invisible and a see-through cover passes your clicks to the desktop. Clicking, double-clicking and right-clicking should work as normal. If anything feels off, turn this back off.
