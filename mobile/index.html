Using it as a wallpaper
A website can't change your phone's home screen by itself. Two ways to get close:
• Keep it open: tap Add to Home Screen in your browser menu, open it from there (full screen, no browser bars), pick a scene, and turn on Keep screen awake. It makes a great desk or charging-stand display.
• Make it your wallpaper: on Android, record your screen while a scene plays (the built-in screen recorder), then set that video as a live wallpaper with any "video wallpaper" app. iPhone can't run live wallpapers, but you can record a short clip and save a frame as a still wallpaper.
Everything runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
